# TASK 1: UNDERSTANDING THE PROBLEM

## 1. What's the problem we are trying to solve?

The idea behind building Nirman is to help people create a home that truly satisfies them.

Everyone loves their home. For most people, building a house is one of the biggest decisions of their lives. Everyone imagines how their dream house should look, how its rooms should feel, how the interiors should be arranged and how the entire house should reflect their personality.

But there is a gap between what people imagine and what actually gets built.

People have ideas, but they often struggle to explain those ideas to an architect. They look at 2D floor plans but cannot properly imagine how the finished house will look or feel. They may have to accept certain design decisions simply because they cannot visualize or communicate the changes they want.

**"IDEAS NEVER BECOME REALITY."**

This is where Nirman comes in.

## 2. What is Nirman?

Nirman is an AI-powered architectural assistant.

**NIRMAN IS A PLACE WHERE YOU CAN PUT YOUR IDEAS ON THE BOARD.**

A place where you can shape your house according to your imagination. A place where you can explore, experience and modify your future home before seeing it on the actual plot.

Nirman aims to bridge the gap between what people imagine and what they finally build.

It gives homeowners the ability to explore their house in an interactive environment, understand their designs, experiment with changes and work alongside their architect.

The goal is not to replace architects. The goal is to make the entire home-design experience more interactive, understandable and satisfying for homeowners.

# 3. HOW DOES NIRMAN WORK?

## Step 1: Login and choose your role

When a user enters Nirman, they get a login page where they can choose between two roles:

**1. Architect**

For professionals who design and manage house projects.

**2. Dreamer**

For people who want to build their dream home and experience the design process themselves.

Both roles get different dashboards because their responsibilities and requirements are different.

## Step 2: Architect creates a project

When an architect logs in, they get an architect dashboard where they can create and manage projects.

To create a project, the architect enters the necessary details:

- Project name
- City and exact plot location
- Plot width
- Plot length
- Road width
- Plot zoning / land use, if known
- Number of floors
- North direction

These details help Nirman understand the project, identify the applicable building regulations and establish the initial design constraints.

The architect can also upload a rough 2D sketch of the house, if available.

The rough sketch is optional. If uploaded, Nirman uses it as a reference for the initial design rather than treating it as a final approved plan.

The architect also defines the project requirements and limitations that should be respected during the design process.

## Step 3: Invite the Dreamer

After creating the project, the architect invites the Dreamer (homeowner) to join the workspace.

The architect pays to activate the project workspace.

Once activated, both the architect and Dreamer get access to the same collaborative environment.

This is where Nirman starts operating as an AI architectural assistant and an interactive home-design platform.

## Step 4: The shared workspace

The workspace is the central part of Nirman.

It is not supposed to look like an ordinary project-management dashboard. It should feel like an interactive environment where the Dreamer can explore and shape their future home.

The workspace contains:

### A. 2D Floor Plan

The existing 2D floor plan is displayed here.

The Dreamer can:
- Understand the complete floor layout.
- Select rooms and design elements.
- Modify room dimensions.
- Change door and window positions and dimensions.
- Explore multiple floors.
- See measurements and other relevant information.

### B. 3D House View

The 3D model is generated from the same house data as the 2D floor plan.

Any valid change made in the 2D plan should automatically reflect in the 3D model.

Similarly, changes made through the 3D environment should update the 2D plan.

The Dreamer can experience the house through three different ways:

**1. Outside View — Free Camera**

The user can freely move the camera around the exterior of the house.

They can rotate, zoom in, zoom out and explore the house from different angles.

They can also select certain elements, such as walls, doors and windows, to make permitted modifications.

**2. Inside View — First-Person Walkthrough**

The user can enter the house and explore it from an eye-level perspective.

They can walk through rooms, look around and understand how the spaces will feel from inside the house.

They can also select editable elements and make permitted changes.

**3. Design View**

The user can work directly with the 2D floor plan while watching the corresponding changes happen in the 3D model.

The purpose is to make designing a house feel interactive and enjoyable, almost like playing a house-building game.

## Step 5: Nirman AI — The intelligent assistant

Nirman AI is an important part of the workspace.

It is not just an ordinary chatbot that answers questions. It understands the house design, the project information and the limitations defined by the architect.

The Dreamer can interact with Nirman through text or voice.

For example:

- "Can we make this bedroom bigger?"
- "Can we increase the kitchen window?"
- "Can we move this wall?"
- "Can we change the layout of this floor?"

Whenever the Dreamer requests a modification, Nirman first checks whether that change is possible within the architect-defined limitations and applicable building constraints.

If the change is possible, Nirman can prepare and apply the proposed modification after the appropriate confirmation.

If the change is not possible, Nirman explains the reason and, wherever possible, suggests an alternative.

The same validation process applies when the Dreamer makes changes manually through the 2D or 3D editor.

For example, if the Dreamer tries to move a wall beyond the permitted boundary, Nirman should prevent that modification and explain the limitation.

**Nirman acts as a validation layer between the user's imagination and the actual house design.**

## Step 6: Collaboration between Architect and Dreamer

Nirman provides a shared workspace where the architect and Dreamer can work on the same house design.

Both can see the latest design and its modifications.

The Dreamer can:
- Explore the house.
- Suggest modifications.
- Share feedback.
- Discuss design alternatives.
- Review updated versions.

The architect can:
- Review the Dreamer's modifications and suggestions.
- Define and update design limitations.
- Make professional design decisions.
- Review and approve proposed changes.
- Update the house design.

Nirman keeps the design and its associated discussions connected, so feedback can be understood in the context of the actual house rather than getting lost in separate conversations.

The purpose is not to create another ordinary messaging application. It is to make communication about the house design more meaningful.

## Step 7: Design history and project memory

Nirman maintains the important information related to the project.

This includes:
- Original project requirements.
- Current house design.
- Previous design versions.
- Modifications made by the Dreamer.
- Architect's changes and decisions.
- Feedback and discussions.
- Important design limitations.

This allows both users to understand how the house has evolved and revisit earlier designs when required.

# 4. THE CORE IDEA BEHIND NIRMAN

Nirman combines three important experiences:

**1. INTERACTIVE HOUSE DESIGN**

A game-like environment where users can explore and modify their future house through 2D and 3D interactions.

**2. AI ARCHITECTURAL ASSISTANCE**

An AI assistant that understands the house, checks proposed changes against defined limitations and helps users explore possible alternatives.

**3. ARCHITECT–DREAMER COLLABORATION**

A shared environment where homeowners can express their ideas, explore their designs and work with their architects.

# 5. THE FINAL VISION

Nirman is not just a floor-plan editor, a 3D visualization tool or a chatbot.

It is a platform where people can experience their future home before it is built.

A place where homeowners are not limited to looking at drawings and imagining what their house might look like.

They can enter their design, explore it, experiment with it and participate in shaping it.

The architect brings professional knowledge and design expertise. The Dreamer brings imagination, preferences and personal requirements. Nirman connects both through an intelligent and interactive environment.

**NIRMAN — WHERE YOUR IDEAS FIND THEIR SHAPE.**
